Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order Cancellation

Customers should contact Payal Gift Hamper as soon as possible if they want to cancel an order.

Certain customised, personalised, perishable or specially prepared products may not be eligible for return or cancellation after preparation. Any applicable conditions will be communicated before confirmation.

Contact

For cancellation, refund or product-related issues, contact us through our Contact Us page.
