Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30 September 2026

Payal Gift Hamper respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used for processing your orders.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address
  • PIN code
  • State
  • Products ordered
  • Order amount

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Process and confirm your order
  • Contact you regarding your order
  • Arrange delivery
  • Respond to customer-support requests
  • Maintain order records

WhatsApp Orders

When you choose to place an order through WhatsApp, the order information entered on the website may be transferred to WhatsApp so that the order can be communicated and confirmed.

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee complete security.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as WhatsApp for communication. Their use of information is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.
